Welcome to North Star Bakery

A Neighborhood Bakery, Baked Fresh Daily

North Star Bakery has been baking fresh bread and pastries in the heart of the neighborhood for over fifteen years. Stop in for a warm loaf on your way to work, or pre-order a cake for your next family gathering.

What We're Known For

  • Naturally leavened sourdough, baked every morning
  • Buttery croissants and seasonal pastries
  • Custom cakes for birthdays, weddings, and community events
