About North Star Bakery

Our Story

North Star Bakery started in a small kitchen with one oven and a simple idea: bake bread the slow way, and let the neighborhood decide the rest. Fifteen years later, we're still mixing, shaping, and baking every loaf by hand.

Our Sourcing

We mill a portion of our flour locally and source butter and seasonal fruit from regional farms whenever we can. If it isn't good enough for our own table, it isn't good enough for yours.
